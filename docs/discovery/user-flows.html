Ecobex Userflow

How a person gets from installing Ecobex to reading their footprint, contributing to a climate project, joining the leaderboard and changing settings.

Scope

Scope

Which flows were mapped, for whom, and what they were mapped from.

Starting point

New product

Mapped from extension-plan-and-factors 5b895d2.

The product

A Chrome extension that measures digital habits (streaming, AI prompts, browsing), shows the estimated CO2 and water they cause as ranges, and points people to offsetting. For v0 only its frontend screens are built, as web pages with sample data and no backend.

For whom, and where

Anyone browsing

Global, English-language, Chrome Web Store, used on a desktop computer

Flows asked for

F1 Sign up and onboarding, F2 Check my metrics, F3 Offset my impact, F4 Leaderboard, F5 Settings

Flows

Flows

Five flows for one role, all planned. Three decisions block building: the project rules still say no accounts, the onboarding needs a consent step the Chrome Web Store requires, and no offset provider is chosen yet.

One flow per goal and persona. The happy path runs across; each decision branches beneath it, and every path ends. A chart wider than the page scrolls sideways inside its frame.

Start and endStepDecisionLeavesStuck

Sign up and onboarding F1 User flow Planned

For

Person who installs Ecobex

Goal
  • Create an account and set up Ecobex, then land on the dashboard
When

Ecobex has just been installed from the Chrome Web Store

Measure

Seven screens from the welcome tab to the dashboard, two of them skippable; target under two minutes, to be tested

Happy path: 7 steps

Acceptance criteria (3)
  • Given a new install, when the person finishes or skips every step, then they land on Metrics and the next visit opens Metrics directly.
  • Given the consent screen, when the person chooses Not now, then no usage is recorded and reopening the welcome tab returns to the consent screen.
  • Given the sign-up form, when the email has no @, then the form stays on the step and says the email needs an @.

Check my metrics F2 User flow Planned

For

Person who installs Ecobex

Goal
  • See the CO2 and water of their digital habits as ranges, by category and over time
When

Wants to know what their digital habits cost the planet this week

Measure

CO₂ and water ranges readable on arrival without scrolling; any category in one click

Happy path: 5 steps

Acceptance criteria (3)
  • Given a person on their first day, when they open Metrics, then the totals show as ranges labelled ’Sample’ and no % change is shown.
  • Given Metrics on 7 days, when the person opens Video calls, then it shows ’not estimated yet’ and no number.
  • Given any figure on Metrics, when the person opens ’How is this calculated?’, then the factor, its range and its source link from factors.js are shown.

Contribute to a climate project F3 User flow Planned

For

Person who installs Ecobex

Goal
  • Find ways to offset, seeing provider, price per tonne and standard before leaving for the provider’s site
When

Sees their footprint and wants to do something about what they cannot reduce

Measure

Provider, price per tonne and standard seen before leaving; three clicks from the side navigation to the provider’s page

Happy path: 5 steps

Acceptance criteria (3)
  • Given the projects list, when the person reads any card, then it shows provider, standard, removal or avoidance, price per tonne and the cost for their last 30 days.
  • Given a project summary, when the person clicks Continue to provider, then the provider’s page opens in a new tab and no payment happens in Ecobex.
  • Given a recorded contribution, when the person opens Metrics, then the footprint is unchanged and the contribution is listed separately.

Leaderboard F4 User flow Planned

For

Person who installs Ecobex

Goal
  • Compare their week-on-week change with others on a leaderboard they choose to join
When

Curious how their week compares with others

Measure

Joined and ranked in two steps; own position always visible

Happy path: 3 steps

Acceptance criteria (3)
  • Given a person who has not joined, when they open Leaderboard, then they see only sample players and a Join button, and no data of theirs.
  • Given a person in their first week, when they join, then their row says ’Not ranked yet’.
  • Given a joined person, when they turn off leaderboard visibility in Settings, then their row disappears from the board.

Settings F5 User flow Planned

For

Person who installs Ecobex

Goal
  • Change what is tracked, their goal, profile and visibility; pause tracking; clear data and sign out
When

Wants to change what Ecobex tracks, how they appear, or to stop tracking for a while

Measure

Any setting changed in two clicks from Settings, saved without a separate Save step

Happy path: 4 steps

Acceptance criteria (3)
  • Given tracking paused for 1 hour, when the hour ends, then tracking resumes and Metrics says so once.
  • Given Clear data, when the person confirms by typing CLEAR, then nothing Ecobex stored remains in the browser and the welcome tab opens.
  • Given a habit switched off, when the person opens Metrics, then past figures for that habit stay in history and no new usage is added.

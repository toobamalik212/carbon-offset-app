# Ecobex competitive analysis: Chrome extensions

Updated 20 Nov 2026 with 13 products, the owner's screenshots and a manual-calculator baseline. Every fact below comes from a public page named in the "Sources" section, or from the owner's screenshots taken on 20 Nov 2026; items resting on a search snippet, a third-party page or a screenshot are marked as such. Anything marked UNVERIFIED could not be confirmed. **We did not install or run any extension ourselves.** The owner's screenshots show products in use, but we could not check them against the live products.

## 1. Scope

| Question | Answer |

## 2. Headline findings

1. **The category is small.** The largest by users is Pebble (about 1,000), then Offset AI (482) and AI Wattch (388). Most have under 40 users (store listings, read 17 to 20 Nov 2026).
2. **Seven of the 13 track AI prompts only.** Five more track browsing or data volume. GreenHelper's scope is unknown. None we could verify combines AI, streaming and video calls, and no source we read covers video calls at all.
3. **Six of the 13 show water.** Offset AI, Airth, Prompt Power, Carbon AI Hub, ByteThirst and GreenQuery. Browsing trackers show CO₂ only, as far as we could see.
4. **The per-prompt numbers disagree by up to about ten times.** Water per AI prompt ranges from 0.26 mL (Google) to roughly 3 mL. The 3 mL end is our computation from rounded figures in the owner's screenshots and is unverified, and the ratio depends on which source is the low end. See section 5.
5. **Few explain their method, and fewer let you change it.** Airth publishes a method page with ranges and overrides. GreenQuery lets users edit its constants. AI Wattch and ByteThirst publish theirs. Offset AI's methodology page returned a 404.
6. **Offset steps are thin.** Offset AI and Airth name providers but state no verification standard. Offset Mode donates to a registered charity with a $1 minimum (per screenshot). Carbon Offset promises trees with no named partner. Most others have none.
7. **Account rules split the field.** Offset AI and Carbon AI Hub need an account to see data. Airth (for its popup), GreenQuery, Prompt Power and (per a snippet) ByteThirst say they work without one. Offset Mode's account status is unverified.

## 5. The numbers disagree

Water per AI text prompt, as stated or computed:

| Source | Water per prompt | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Google (Gemini median prompt) | 0.26 mL | Google's blog post, Aug 2025; not independently verified |
| OpenAI CEO's essay | about 0.32 mL | 0.000085 gallons, converted by us |
| Airth | 0.89 mL (typical text prompt) | Airth's methodology page |
| Offset AI | roughly 2.7 to 3 mL | Computed by us from the owner's screenshots, using rounded figures: 0.4 L for 141 prompts, 0.5 L for 168 prompts, and 0.4 L for 150 ChatGPT prompts |

The ratio depends on the low end: about 10 times against Google's figure, about 8 to 9 times against the OpenAI CEO's, and about 3 times against Airth's. The screenshot inputs are rounded to one digit, so the true spread could be wider. Energy per prompt is closer: about 0.33 to 0.34 Wh in Offset AI's screenshots (46.3 Wh over 141 prompts, 55.3 Wh over 168, 51 Wh over 150 ChatGPT prompts, also computed by us), against 0.24 Wh from Google and 0.34 Wh quoted by the OpenAI CEO. Offset AI's energy figure matches the OpenAI one, so the tenfold gap is in water, not energy. The spread suggests different tools count different things as "water", possibly including water used to generate the electricity. We could not confirm that. For Ecobex it supports two choices: show ranges, and say what is counted.

## 6. The flow: onboarding, popup, dashboard, offset

All items marked "screenshot" are from the owner's screenshots, taken 20 Nov 2026, and are not independently verified.

### Onboarding and account
- **Offset AI:** install, create an account to see emissions over time, then offset (site). Sign-in is email and password, with "Forgot password", "Sign up" and "Create a team account" (screenshot). The popup says "Connected as" an obscured email and "No conversation detected. Open ChatGPT to begin tracking" (screenshot).
- **Airth:** the popup works with zeros before sign-in and shows a banner "Save your stats and offset your impact, Sign in to sync across devices" (screenshot). The sign-in page offers "Continue with Google" or an email magic link, and its address carries the source "extension" (screenshot). The privacy page says local data stays on the device unless you sign in.
- **Carbon AI Hub:** sign in or "Start tracking free", add to Chrome, optionally connect OpenAI or Anthropic usage (site).
- **GreenQuery and Prompt Power:** no account (store listings). ByteThirst's free tier needs no account (Product Hunt snippet only). Offset Mode: account status UNVERIFIED.
- **Others:** no onboarding was described anywhere we could read.

### Toolbar popup
- **Offset AI** (screenshot): tabs for ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity (red dots beside Claude and Perplexity; meaning not visible), a prompt count, Carbon and Water tiles, "See Dashboard" and "About this extension".
- **Airth** (screenshot): sign-in banner, Today and All time counts, a "Your Footprint" block (CO₂, kWh, water) with equivalents (smartphone charges, minutes of LED bulb), a four-week chart, a "Go Pro" upsell and a one-off "Offset it now" button.
- **Offset Mode** (screenshot): a forest-photo background with two large numbers (data used, grams of carbon), then a white donation panel with a single "Donate $1.00" button.
- **GreenQuery** (screenshot): dark popup with Overview, Analytics, History or Tools, Settings and Offset tabs; totals for water and CO₂; a one-line status message; six equivalents (bottles, phone charges, showers, driving, bathtubs, tree-years).
- **GreenHelper** (screenshot): a chat assistant with five suggested questions and an icon rail (home, analytics, checklist, trophy, chat, help, profile, settings).

### Dashboard
- **Offset AI** (screenshots): "Personal dashboard" with time tabs (7 Days, 30 Days, 90 Days, This Month, This Year, Custom), an offset banner ("You've generated 0.026 kg CO2e this month. Start offsetting it."), four tiles (Prompts, Energy, CO₂e, Water) each with a percent change and sparkline, a tracked-tools table and a donut. ## 7. The manual behaviour to beat

Four public web tools were read as page text on 20 Nov 2026. We submitted nothing. Result screens are UNVERIFIED for all four.

| Tool | What the person enters | Shows | Offsetting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website Carbon Calculator (Wholegrain Digital) | One page address | CO₂ per page view only | None |
| EcoTree digital CO₂ calculator | Monthly streaming hours, total internet hours, connection type, number of screens (as described on the page) | Monthly CO₂ only, "estimates based on averages", no formula or sources | Yes: credits of 1 tonne each, certified by Bureau Veritas, listed at EUR 72, 143.75 and 75 per tonne |
| Omni Calculator, AI water footprint | AI model, queries per day (default 8), number of users | Water and energy per query, day and year, as showers and kettles; CO₂ not stated on the page | None |
| Nos Gestes Climat (ADEME) | A full personal-footprint questionnaire; the digital questions and question count were not readable | CO₂e and water | None mentioned |

What this tells us:
- To cover streaming, AI and websites, a person needs at least two or three separate tools, and each asks them to recall hours or counts from memory.
- None that we could see covers video calls, keeps history, or measures real usage. Result screens were not read for any of the four, and Nos Gestes Climat's digital questions were not read.
- None combines CO₂, water and offsetting. EcoTree has CO₂ and offsetting, Omni has water and energy, Website Carbon has CO₂ only.
- Price benchmark: EcoTree's listed EUR 72 to 143.75 per tonne is about EUR 0.07 to 0.14 per kg (our arithmetic). Airth's screenshot shows about £0.10 per kg. Both are single sources and the currencies differ, so treat this as an order of magnitude only.
- Effort per tool in minutes was not timed.

## 8. Gaps we could aim at (our interpretation, not a sourced fact)

1. **Breadth:** AI, streaming and video calls together. None of the products we could verify does this (GreenHelper is wholly unverified). Caution: our own figures file marks video calls "not estimated yet", because we found no solid per-hour source. Breadth is only a promise we can keep for AI and streaming today.
2. **Visible method across more than AI:** the open methods we found cover AI prompts. A method page that also covers streaming, with ranges and sources, would match the best of them.
3. **Water beside CO₂ for non-AI activity:** none of the browsing trackers shows water.
4. **An offset step that shows its working:** the provider, the price per tonne and the standard on the screen before leaving for the provider's site. We did not find all three on any competitor page we could read, though several pages were unopened. The manual tool EcoTree does state a named project, a price per tonne and a certifier (section 7). This also matches our rule that Ecobex only links out to an external, verified provider and takes no payments.
5. **Value without an account:** four competitors say they work without one (Airth for its popup, GreenQuery, Prompt Power and, from a snippet, ByteThirst), so this alone does not set us apart. It is a baseline.

## 9. Unique proposition: options and a recommendation

You asked for a unique proposition. Three options follow, each tied to the gaps above. These are our hypotheses, not validated with users.

| Option | Idea | Evidence for | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Whole digital day | One place for AI, streaming and calls, in CO₂ and water | None of the products we could verify covers all three (section 2) | We have no sourced video-call figure yet |
| B. Numbers you can check | Every figure shown as a range, with the method and editable assumptions one tap away | Airth, GreenQuery and ByteThirst publish or expose their methods; water per prompt differs by up to about ten times across tools (section 5, with the screenshot-derived caveat there) | Competitors already do this for AI; we must do it for more activities |
| C. Offset with receipts | Before you leave for the provider, see who, how much per tonne and which standard | Not found together on the competitor pages we could read (section 6); the manual tool EcoTree states all three (section 7) | We do not process payments, so the standard is the provider's to prove |

**Recommendation:** lead with B and C, and say A only as far as we can back it. Draft:

*Ecobex shows the CO₂ and water of your digital habits as ranges, with every assumption visible, and shows you who you would offset with, at what price and to which standard, before you leave for their site.*

Why this order, and what it still needs:
- **B** is partly in place: `dashboard/src/data/factors.js` already holds ranges, sources and confidence for AI prompts and streaming. "Editable assumptions" is **not** in PLAN.md today, so that part would be a new decision, and a method page is already planned as Step 11.
- **C** is not ready: no provider has been chosen, and `factors.js` holds no price per tonne or standard. PLAN.md Step 10 only links out. The price and standard content is **to be confirmed** until a verified provider is chosen and sourced.
- **A** needs a sourced video-call figure first. When we have one, the line can grow to "your whole digital day".

**This differs from the proposition already confirmed in PLAN.md** (Nov 2026), which leads with the whole digital day across AI, streaming and calls. The reasons for the difference: the new evidence shows the method and offset gaps are open to us now, and video calls are still marked "not estimated yet". If you prefer to keep the confirmed line, it still holds; it just promises more than we can show today.

# Ecobex competitive analysis: Chrome extensions

Read between 17 and 18 Nov 2026. Every fact below comes from a public page named in the "Sources" section; items that rest on a search snippet or a third-party page are marked as such. Anything marked **UNVERIFIED** could not be confirmed, and the list of what could not be opened is at the end. **No extension was installed or tried**, so everything here comes from store listings, vendor sites and the owner's screenshot, not from using the products.

## 1. Scope (agreed before research)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Our offering | Ecobex: a Chrome extension that measures digital habits, shows estimated CO₂ and water, and points to offsetting. For now we design only its frontend screens. |
| Market and region | Global, English-language, Chrome Web Store |
| Number of competitors | 8 (6 to 8 was chosen) |
| Flow compared | Onboarding, then dashboard, then offset step |

## 2. Headline findings

1. **The category is small.** The largest competitor found has 482 users and 8 ratings; most have under 40 users (store listings, read 17 and 18 Nov 2026).
2. **Nobody covers AI, streaming and video calls together.** Five of the eight are AI-prompt trackers. One groups browsing time into categories including Streaming. None mentions video calls.
3. **Water is shown by about half.** Four of the eight show it or claim to (see matrix).
4. **A credible offset step is rare.** One competitor names offset partners but states no price or verification standard. One promises trees with no named partner, price or proof. For the rest we found none (Carbon AI Hub's site links a Carbon Credits page we did not read).
5. **Few explain their method.** AI Wattch and ByteThirst publish how they calculate, and Prompt Power's listing cites the research behind its rates. Offset AI's methodology page returned a 404.
6. **Privacy claims are the norm, but some sit awkwardly with the data they declare.** Several say "local only" while declaring user activity or web history as collected data types, or running analytics by default.

## 5. The flow: onboarding, dashboard, offset

### Onboarding
- **Offset AI** (site, offsetai.app): install the extension, create an account to see emissions and water over time, then choose when and how to offset. First-run screens: UNVERIFIED.
- **Carbon AI Hub** (site): sign in or "Start tracking free", add to Chrome, optionally connect OpenAI or Anthropic API usage, then messages are logged automatically. An account is required.
- **ByteThirst**: free tier needs no account, per a Product Hunt snippet only. First run UNVERIFIED.
- **Prompt Power** (store listing): "No accounts". First run UNVERIFIED.
- **AI Wattch, Carbon Footprint Tracker, Sustainarian, Carbon Offset**: no onboarding described anywhere we could read. UNVERIFIED.

**What this means:** only two competitors (Offset AI and Carbon AI Hub) describe an onboarding flow, and both ask for an account. Two more (ByteThirst and Prompt Power) say no account is needed but describe no first run. Nobody's first-run experience could be inspected.

### Dashboard
- **Offset AI** (owner's screenshot, not independently verified): sidebar with Dashboard, Offset Projects, Offset your impact, Account, Help center; time tabs 7 Days, 30 Days, 90 Days, This Month, This Year, Custom; an offset banner; four KPI cards (Prompts, Energy, CO₂e, Water) with change and sparklines; a table of tracked AI tools; an AI Tools Mix donut.
- **Carbon AI Hub** (listing, site): daily trends, breakdown by AI tool, CSV reports, tips, units such as trees, kettles and km.
- **ByteThirst** (homepage): free tier has daily and weekly views and impact cards; paid adds 30, 60 and 90-day trends, spike alerts and a breakdown; its popup shows daily and weekly footprint with volume conversions such as teaspoons.
- **Carbon Footprint Tracker** (listing): hourly, daily, weekly and monthly charts; counters for current tab, session, custom period and lifetime; top 10 sites; content-type breakdown.
- **Sustainarian** (listing): daily, weekly and monthly views; six categories; equivalents (latte, car travel, red meat) and tips.
- **Prompt Power** (listing): platform breakdown and interaction history; comparisons to a hair dryer, TV time and Google searches.
- **AI Wattch** (GitHub): real-time metrics, session summaries, model comparisons, daily summaries, tips.
- **Carbon Offset**: a progress and community trees counter. Layout UNVERIFIED.

Actual screens, charts and layout were UNVERIFIED for every competitor except Offset AI's (screenshot only). We never saw a competitor's screens.

### Offset step
- **Offset AI** (site): funds climate and water projects, partners ClimeCo and We Are Neutral. Price, project list and verification standard are UNVERIFIED.
- **Carbon Offset** (listing): "planting three trees per user every year" and a counter of trees planted by all users. No partner, cost, certification or proof is shown.
- **ByteThirst** (privacy page): "a portion of our revenue" goes to Stripe Climate. No percentage, no user-facing purchase.
- **Carbon AI Hub**: none in the extension; the site links a Carbon Credits page we did not read.
- **AI Wattch, Prompt Power, Carbon Footprint Tracker, Sustainarian**: none found.

## 6. The manual behaviour to beat

**This part of the brief is not delivered.** We did not research a specific habit calculator. The only manual tool that surfaced was the Website Carbon Calculator (Wholegrain Digital), which estimates the carbon of one website, not a person's habits, and we did not open its page. The manual baseline (typing your hours of streaming, calls and prompts into a calculator, or doing nothing) is UNVERIFIED and should be researched before we claim anything about it. One related observation comes from our earlier research page (a private artifact, https://claude.ai/artifact/KskLC7C3Twe7nj2ZVWbXjE, not in the repo): the per-use figures we gathered for streaming, video calls and AI prompts disagree widely between sources, so a calculator's answer likely depends on which figures it uses. That is our inference, not something we checked in any calculator.

## 7. Gaps we could aim at (our interpretation, not a sourced fact)

1. **Breadth:** no competitor combines AI prompts, streaming and video calls. This is the clearest gap in what we read.
2. **Trust:** AI Wattch and ByteThirst publish their method, and ByteThirst already publishes a low/mid/high table with limits. Whether the other competitors show ranges is unverified, so ranges alone are not a proven gap. A clear "How we calculate" page with sources is still worth building to match the best of them.
3. **Water and CO₂ together:** we did not find water for AI Wattch, Carbon Footprint Tracker, Sustainarian or Carbon Offset.
4. **An honest offset step:** the offset steps we found are either vague (trees, no partner) or lack a stated price and verification standard.
5. **No account to get first value:** the two competitors that describe a flow both require an account; two others say none is needed.

## 8. Draft unique proposition (for you to confirm or change)

*Ecobex shows the CO₂ and water of your whole digital day, not just your AI chats, as estimates with the method and sources visible, and takes you to a verified offset provider when you're ready.*

This is a hypothesis built from section 7. It is not validated with users, and the "whole digital day" part depends on what the extension can actually measure later (see PLAN.md, "Later").

## 9. What could not be verified

- No extension was installed, so onboarding, popups, first-run screens and real dashboards are unverified for all eight.
- Store screenshots could not be viewed for any of them (blank or unreadable).
- Chrome Web Store permission lists were not visible for any competitor; AI Wattch's came from a third-party site (extscope.org).
- Offset AI: dashboard needs a login and was not opened; its methodology page returned 404; offset price, projects and verification standard are not stated.
- ByteThirst: store page loaded empty; user and rating counts conflict between sources; one third-party site marked it "Suspected delisted".
- AI Wattch: licence is MIT on GitHub and MPL 2.0 on Firefox Add-ons; it also has a second, apparently different, listing that was not assessed.
- Carbon Footprint Tracker: the "100 g CO₂ per GB" figure came from a search summary only, not the listing. The listing says "open source" but links no repository.
- Carbon AI Hub: scopelocal.co.uk was blocked; the Carbon Credits page was not read.
- Business models are unverified for AI Wattch, Prompt Power, Carbon Footprint Tracker, Sustainarian and Carbon Offset.
- The competitor search was not exhaustive. Other tools named in search results and **not** researched: EcoPrompt, AI Water Footprint, Footprint AI, PromptFootprint, Carbon-Karma, WebCarbon, Carbonalyser (mainly Firefox), Offset Mode, refoorest.
- Mobbin (flow screenshots) failed to connect, so no screen-flow library was used.
- Privacy policies were read only for ByteThirst and Carbon AI Hub. For the other six, privacy and "tracks" claims rest on store listings. Sustainarian's policy (a Google Drive link), Carbon Offset's policy and Prompt Power's policy and developer details were not opened. Carbon AI Hub's listing mixes policy links and emails from two domains (scopelocal.co.uk and anivault.co.uk).
- extensionauditor.com returned 403 for Prompt Power and Offset AI. The extpose.com page for Sustainarian and developer pages were not opened. Carbon Offset's user count is not shown on its listing.
- ByteThirst: a third-party site (extscope) claims offset purchases; we could not confirm that, and the manifest and delisting status are unverified. AI Wattch: its repository source and docs beyond the landing page were not read, and the extscope entry is stale (v1.1.16).
- Carbon AI Hub's calculation factors and sources were not seen. Offset AI's business-tier price is to be confirmed.
